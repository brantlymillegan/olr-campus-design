the adaptation.

MODEL 3 CAMPUS DRIVING VEHICLE

Original task-authored geometric interpretation of a current Tesla Model 3,
created specifically for the OLR campus visualization. Red multilayer exterior,
white upholstery, detailed cabin, glass roof, and animated wheel pivots are
modeled from scratch. No downloaded third-party 3D model, image, texture, or
Tesla promotional artwork is embedded in the GLB or editable Blender asset.
The vehicle is an architectural visualization, not a manufacturer CAD model.
Tesla and Model 3 identify the depicted vehicle; this is not an official Tesla asset.

Official visual/dimensional reference sources (viewed October 10, 2026):
https://www.tesla.com/model3
https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/model3/en_us/GUID-56562137-FC31-4110-A13C-9A9FC6657BF0.html
https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/model3/en_us/GUID-6C6C3944-9674-4E81-A0E8-94D60B6D87B9.html
https://www.tesla.com/ownersmanual/model3/en_us/GUID-A5F2B9D0-E7C8-40F5-9642-58F3657B123E.html

Nominal current Model 3 dimensions: 4.720 m length, 1.850 m body width,
2.089 m mirror width, 1.440 m height, 2.875 m wheelbase, 1.584 m track.
Small artistic details may extend slightly beyond nominal manufacturer bounds;
vehicle-spec.json records measured complete-mesh bounds.
