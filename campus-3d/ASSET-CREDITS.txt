Room crucifixes
"Crucifix" by The Hunt Museum.
Original object: rosewood cross with boxwood corpus, probably by Andrea Fantoni
of Bergamo, Italy, according to the museum's catalog description.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Original model:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/crucifix-9e5f9a61cf914e649dec44041affe1d2
Official download record:
https://zenodo.org/records/18948596

Modifications: the freestanding pedestal and unused lower stem were removed
for wall mounting. The intact corpus, feet, decorative finials and INRI plaque
were retained. Geometry was simplified, the original texture atlas was resized
from 8K to 4K and JPEG encoded, and the model was rescaled and oriented above doors inside
27 occupied school rooms. The museum does not endorse this campus design or
the adaptation.

Building 1 entrance shrine — Madonna and Child

"Madonna and child" by Emm (emm / edemaistre).
Creator: https://sketchfab.com/edemaistre

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Original model:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/madonna-and-child-74e4278b29214c7eb0268628477e8bcd
Public metadata:
https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/74e4278b29214c7eb0268628477e8bcd
Downloaded from the Allen Institute for AI Objaverse public mirror:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse/resolve/main/glbs/000-082/74e4278b29214c7eb0268628477e8bcd.glb

Modifications: the scanned rocky terrain base was removed and the underside
was capped. Rear-scalp texture discoloration beneath the crown was neutralized,
while the original pale-stone texture and sculpted detail were retained.
White crowns were added to Mary and the Child, together with a modeled bronze
rosary. The crowned sculpture was uniformly scaled to five feet high, oriented
and mounted in the new Building 1 entrance shrine. The original creator does
not endorse the campus design or adaptation.
